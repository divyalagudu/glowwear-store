# Image sources

GlowWear's catalog images in this package are a mix of:
1. Three blouse photos supplied by the store owner/user in this conversation.
2. Five original GlowWear product-style images cropped from an AI-generated GlowWear storefront mockup.

The web browsing performed during development was used only for visual direction and product-photography inspiration. Third-party commercial-site images were not packaged into the ZIP.

For production, replace demo images with product photography you own or have explicit commercial rights to use.
